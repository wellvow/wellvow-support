WELLVOW App — Support

WELLVOW is the companion app for WELLVOW LED strip controllers. It pairs over Bluetooth LE, provisions the controller onto your Wi‑Fi, and gives you presets, DIY colors, music‑reactive effects, schedules and groups.

Contact

Email: support@wellvow.com — we reply within 2 business days.

Quick help

  • No device yet? On the Add Device screen tap “Try the demo” to explore every screen without hardware.
  • Controller not found: make sure Bluetooth is on, the controller is powered, and no other phone is currently connected to it (only one phone can be connected at a time). Hold the controller button for 3 seconds to reset pairing.
  • Wi‑Fi setup: open the controller in the app → tap the Wi‑Fi bar → choose your 2.4 GHz network. You can also join the controller hotspot WLED-AP (password wled1234) and open http://4.3.2.1 in a browser.
  • Music mode not reacting: in the Music page raise Sensitivity and tap “Calibrate” while the room is quiet.
