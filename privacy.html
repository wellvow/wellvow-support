WELLVOW App Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026 · Applies to the WELLVOW iOS app (bundle ID com.hantang.wellvow)

1. Summary

WELLVOW is an app that controls WELLVOW LED strip controllers and HUB devices over Bluetooth LE and your local Wi‑Fi network. The app does not require an account and does not collect personal information to work. Your device list, effects, schedules and settings are stored only on your phone and inside your controllers.

2. Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or advertising identifiers. We do not use analytics SDKs or advertising SDKs, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

3. Data that may leave your phone

  • Community posts and feedback (optional). If you choose to post in the in‑app Community forum or send feedback, the text you write (and an effect file, if you attach one) is sent to our server so it can be shown to other users or read by our support team. Posts are associated with a random identifier generated on your phone, not with your identity. You can delete your own posts; other users can report and block content.
  • Controller communication. Commands you send to your controllers travel over Bluetooth LE or your local network directly to the device. Wi‑Fi credentials you enter during setup are sent only to your controller and are not sent to us.
  • Firmware updates. When you check for a firmware update, the app sends the controller's model and firmware version to our update server to find the right file.
  • Product and promotion listings. The Products and Deals pages load public listing data from our server; no personal data is sent with these requests.

4. Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth LE to discover and connect to WELLVOW controllers (device names beginning with "WVOW_") and to reconnect automatically when a controller comes back into range, including in the background. Bluetooth is not used to determine your location or to collect any data about you.

5. Data retention and security

Community posts and feedback are kept on our server until you delete them or until we remove them for moderation reasons. Server communication uses HTTPS. Communication with controllers uses a device‑specific key derived from the controller.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Your rights

You may request deletion of community content or feedback you submitted by contacting us at the address below. Users in California, the EU/UK and other regions with privacy laws may exercise their applicable rights through the same contact.

8. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the effective date above.

9. Contact

WELLVOW support: support@wellvow.com

WELLVOW 隐私政策（中文）

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 25 日

WELLVOW App 用于通过蓝牙 BLE 和本地 Wi‑Fi 控制 WELLVOW 灯带控制器与 HUB 设备。App 不需要注册账号，正常使用不收集任何个人信息；设备列表、效果、定时与设置仅保存在您的手机和控制器中。

不收集的数据：姓名、邮箱、电话、精确位置、通讯录、照片、广告标识符；不使用统计分析或广告 SDK，不做跨应用追踪。

可能离开手机的数据：①您主动在社区发帖或提交反馈时输入的文字（以及附带的效果文件）会发送到我们的服务器，帖子仅关联手机随机生成的标识符，不关联您的身份，可自行删除，其他用户可举报、屏蔽；②发给控制器的指令经蓝牙或本地网络直接到达设备，配网时输入的 Wi‑Fi 密码只发给控制器；③检查固件更新时会把控制器型号和固件版本发到更新服务器；④产品与促销页面从服务器读取公开信息，不附带个人数据。

蓝牙：仅用于发现、连接名称以 WVOW_ 开头的自有设备，并在设备回到范围时（含后台）自动重连，不用于定位或收集数据。

联系我们：support@wellvow.com；您可通过此邮箱要求删除您提交的社区内容或反馈。
