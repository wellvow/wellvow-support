WELLVOW – App Review demo video

Filmed on a physical iPhone 11 Pro with the WELLVOW WLED‑STRIP controller and WELLVOW HUB (our own hardware). Duration 3:20. Direct file: review-demo.mp4 (MP4, 720×1280, 25 MB).

Chapters

  1. Add device – the app scans and pairs with the controller over Bluetooth LE (initial pairing)
  2. Wi‑Fi setup – home Wi‑Fi credentials are sent to the controller over BLE
  3. Presets & color sets – tapping a scene changes the physical LED strip; the pen opens 12 color sets
  4. DIY – color wheel and modes applied to the strip in real time
  5. Music reactive – the controller microphone drives the strip while a phone plays music
  6. Smart Color, 7. Wiring, 8. Schedules, 9. My Effects (Studio download over Wi‑Fi)
  7. HUB – capture of a third‑party controller signal, split outputs, Wi‑Fi sync between two strips

Notes for the reviewer: no account is needed. Without hardware, tap “Try the demo” on the Add Device screen to explore every screen. Contact: support@wellvow.com
